# BORN AGAIN — JOHN 3:3

**Who is Jesus Christ? And what does it mean to be born again?**

When Nicodemus, a Pharisee and teacher of Israel, encountered Jesus Christ, he recognized that the signs Jesus performed could only come from God. Yet Christ confronted this religious man with a startling truth:

*"Truly, truly, I say to you, unless one is born again he cannot see the kingdom of God."* — John 3:3 (ESV)

**Born again? How can a man be born a second time, and why would someone as religious as Nicodemus need such a thing?**

Here lies the very heart of the gospel.

Our greatest problem is not a lack of knowledge, morality, or religious devotion, but the condition of our hearts (Ephesians 2:1). How can those who are spiritually dead make themselves alive?

**They cannot.**

What we cannot accomplish for ourselves, God alone can do.

To be born again is not to become a better version of ourselves, but to be given a new heart and new life by the Holy Spirit through the sovereign grace of God (Ezekiel 36:26–27).

Through the death and resurrection of Jesus Christ, sinners are offered forgiveness and eternal life.

This hoodie bears witness to a profound truth: **Salvation is not the achievement of man, but the gracious work of God.**

Have you merely heard of Jesus Christ, or has God opened your eyes to see who He truly is and given you a new heart to trust in Him?
